Our 4-Stage Safety & Quality Checks

Every batch of prints we produce undergoes four critical internal checks before leaving our workshop:

Mechanical & Stress Testing Passed

We perform regular destructive and load testing on sample parts. This ensures excellent layer adhesion so that items won't easily snap, break, or create sharp, hazardous edges under normal use.

Chemical & Material Safety Certified

We source raw filaments and resins exclusively from reputable, accredited suppliers. We maintain full Material Safety Data Sheets (MSDS) to ensure our materials are free from harmful heavy metals or banned toxins.

Dimensional Accuracy Checks Verified

Using digital calipers and high-precision Go/No-Go gauges, we verify that prints perfectly match design tolerances. This eliminates friction hazards or loose fits in moving assemblies.

Thermal Stability Inspection Tested

For items intended for environments exposed to warmth (like automotive parts or tech enclosures), we test the heat deflection points to prevent warping, melting, or failure during operation.
